How do I...

Respond to a client message

  1. Open `Contacts` in the left menu.
  2. Use `Portal Messages` or `Unread Only` to filter.
  3. Select the contact name to open the thread.
  4. Use `Reply to Client` and submit your response.
  5. Optional: attach files with the attachments field.

Update a booking status

  1. Open `Bookings` in the left menu.
  2. Click the couple name to open booking detail.
  3. In `Update Status`, pick the new status.
  4. Click `Update`.
  5. Add context in `Admin Notes` if needed.

Review service requests

  1. Open `Service Requests`.
  2. Filter by request type or status.
  3. Open an item by clicking the requester name.
  4. Update status and save notes on the detail page.

Export operational data

  1. Navigate to `Contacts`, `Bookings`, `Payments`, or `Service Requests`.
  2. Click `Export CSV` in the page header.
  3. Save the generated file and share internally.

Review client inspiration boards

  1. Open `Inspiration Boards`.
  2. Search by client name, email, color palette, or theme.
  3. Open the board to view selected colors, florals, and notes.

Manage admin users (owner only)

  1. Open `Admin Users` from the side menu.
  2. Click `New Admin` to create an account.
  3. Use `Toggle` to activate/deactivate staff accounts.
  4. Never share credentials across multiple people.
